## Getting Help

If you're experiencing an issue or have a question about US Civics Ready, here's how to get help:

### Report a Bug or Request a Feature

Open an issue on GitHub:
https://github.com/badriram/citizenship-test/issues

Please include:
- A description of the problem or suggestion
- Your iOS version and device model
- Steps to reproduce the issue (if applicable)

### Common Questions

**Q: Do I need an internet connection?**
A: No. The app works fully offline. An internet connection is only used to refresh legislator data and resolve your congressional district.

**Q: How do I sync progress between devices?**
A: Make sure iCloud is enabled on both devices. Your study progress will sync automatically via iCloud.

**Q: The app shows the wrong representative or senator.**
A: Legislator data updates automatically every 14 days. You can also check that your state and district are set correctly on the home screen.

**Q: Location access was denied. How do I set my district?**
A: You can enter your 5-digit ZIP code on the home screen to resolve your district, or manually select your state.
