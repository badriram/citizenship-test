# Privacy Policy

**US Civics Ready**
Last updated: March 17, 2026

## Overview

US Civics Ready is a study app for the US citizenship naturalization test. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

## Data We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.** The app has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no user accounts.

## Location Data

The app optionally requests your location to determine your state and congressional district. This is used solely to personalize civics questions (e.g., showing your state's senators and representative).

- Location data is processed on your device and sent only to the U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder API to resolve your congressional district.
- Location data is never stored on any server we operate.
- You can use the app without granting location access by manually selecting your state and entering your ZIP code.

## iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled, your study progress is synced across your devices using Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/).

## Third-Party Services

The app connects to the following external services:

- **U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder** (geocoding.geo.census.gov) — to resolve your congressional district from coordinates
- **GitHub Pages** (unitedstates.github.io) — to download updated legislator data

No personal information is sent to these services beyond what is required for the request (coordinates for district lookup).

## Data Storage

All study progress and app settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If iCloud is enabled, this data syncs to your iCloud account.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
